## Findings that limit this record

1. **The `rust-sandbox` leg is not evidence at this HEAD.** All 777 sandbox calls were `fallback:E-DEP`; zero were served by the guest. The committed guest pin does not match the built guest (D-P25: `ffa7b28a...` committed, `91b0970e...` built), so every sandbox call declines and Python answers. Its 12/12 is Python's answer. A sandbox leg counts only when the guest is served. This is recorded as a blocker for the sandbox flip, not as a pass.
2. **`cargo test -p gsd-adapter -p gsd-sandbox -p gsd-sdk` exits 101 with 19 failing tests**, all in the D-P25 guest-pin cascade: 17 `sandbox_engine::*` unit and integration tests (`guest module bytes do not match the manifest SHA-256 digest`), `gsd_engine_backend::a_read_prints_no_failover_notice_under_any_backend_setting` (the sandbox answers `E-DEP` and the host answers, from the same mismatch), and `guest_digest::built_guest_digest_matches_the_committed_pin`. I did not verify each of the 19 individually beyond the messages above. No test was weakened.
3. The R7 agent's interim records (`interim-64f5759/`) were taken on `53df964`, before D-P25 and before the flip build. They are not evidence for HEAD and are kept only as history.
